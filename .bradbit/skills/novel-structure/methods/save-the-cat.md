# Save the Cat (Blake Snyder)

## What it is
A 15-beat sheet for commercial storytelling, scalable from film to novel.
It is precise about timing and turn points.

## Beats
1. Opening image
2. Theme stated
3. Setup
4. Catalyst
5. Debate
6. Break into two
7. B story
8. Fun and games
9. Midpoint
10. Bad guys close in
11. All is lost
12. Dark night of the soul
13. Break into three
14. Finale
15. Final image

## When it works best
Commercial fiction with a clear protagonist and change arc: thriller,
romcom, commercial drama, YA. Less natural for quiet literary novels or
ensemble stories without a single arc.
