# Chapter outline

## Goal
Turn the chosen method into a chapter map before drafting prose.

## Chapter format
```markdown
## Chapter N — <working title>
**POV:** ...
**Time and place:** ...
**Dramatic function:** what this chapter accomplishes.
**What happens:** 2-4 lines.
**What changes:** the turn, decision or revelation.
**Ends with:** hook or transition.
```

## Process
1. Read `structure/chosen-method.md` and the whole bible.
2. Group beats into chapters; each chapter needs a function and a change.
3. Check POV distribution and pacing across acts.
4. Save `structure/outline.md`.
5. Confirm the outline with the user before drafting chapters.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Capítulo 7 — El margen de la carta:** POV Julia. Noche en el taller.
> Función: primera prueba de que las cartas son auténticas. Termina con
> la firma que reconoce.

## Do not
- Do not write dialogue or full prose in the outline.
- Do not create chapters without a clear function or change.
