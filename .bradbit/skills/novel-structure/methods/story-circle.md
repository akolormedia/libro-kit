# Story Circle (Dan Harmon)

## What it is
A circular simplification of the hero's journey in 8 steps, useful for
contained stories and strong character arcs.

## Steps
1. A character is in a zone of comfort
2. But they want something
3. They enter an unfamiliar situation
4. They adapt to it
5. They get what they wanted
6. They pay a heavy price
7. They return to their familiar situation
8. Having changed

## When it works best
Short novels, novellas, character-driven stories and books with a clear
single arc. Also useful to check that every act has a real change.
