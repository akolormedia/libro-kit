# Classic three-act structure

## What it is
The most basic model: setup, confrontation, resolution. It works as a
skeleton for almost any more detailed method.

## Acts
1. **Setup (~25%):** world, character, inciting incident. End of act 1 =
   a decision the protagonist cannot undo.
2. **Confrontation (~50%):** rising obstacles, a midpoint that raises the
   stakes, everything complicates until the lowest point.
3. **Resolution (~25%):** climax, facing the central conflict, resolution
   and a new status quo.

## When it works best
Fast planning, or as a top layer before a more granular method. Useful
when the book has a clear protagonist and a single central conflict.
