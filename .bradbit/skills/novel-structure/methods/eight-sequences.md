# Eight-sequence structure

## What it is
A film-derived method that divides the story into 8 blocks of roughly
equal length, each with its own mini-arc (setup, complication, turn).

## Sequences
1. Status quo + inciting incident
2. Reaction, decision to act
3. First attempts in the new world/plan
4. Midpoint — raise the stakes
5. Complications, the plan goes wrong
6. Everything falls apart
7. Regroup, final plan
8. Climax and resolution

## When it works best
Plot-driven novels and books with clear act breaks. Each sequence can map
to a group of chapters, which makes pacing easier to audit.
