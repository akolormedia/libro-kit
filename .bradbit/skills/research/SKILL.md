# Research

## Goal
Ground the book in verifiable detail without turning it into a lecture.

## Process
1. Define the question and which chapter/character needs it.
2. Search direct and recent sources; prioritize primary sources.
3. Take notes in your own words; cite title + link.
4. Separate verified fact, reported usage and your interpretation.
5. Save in `research/<topic>.md` with a date.
6. Summarize only what changes the scene; keep the rest as reference.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Hallazgo:** en el siglo XVII, la tinta ferrogálica pardea con el
> tiempo; una carta "azul" sería anacrónica salvo que se explique.
> **Fuente:** ...

## Do not
- Do not present an assumption as research.
- Do not reproduce long passages.
- Do not let research delay the draft; mark `[NOTA: verificar]` and keep
  writing.
