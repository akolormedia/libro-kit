# Idea development

## Goal
Turn an idea into a writable premise: logline, theme, genre, audience,
comparable titles, target length, point of view and tense.

## Process
1. Gather what exists: genre, audience, format, length, influences,
   constraints and any decision already made.
2. Explore 3-5 premise directions if the idea is open. For each: a
   one-line logline, the human tension, and why it can sustain a book.
3. Define:
   - Logline (protagonist, want, obstacle, stakes)
   - Theme (the human question, without moralizing)
   - Genre and tone
   - Audience and comparable titles
   - Target word count
   - Point of view and tense
4. Write the result to `bible/premise.md`.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Logline:** una restauradora de libros descubre en un misal del siglo
> XVII las cartas de una mujer acusada de herejía y debe decidir si
> publicarlas o proteger a su familia.
> **Tema:** qué precio tiene la verdad cuando contradice a quienes amas.

## Do not
- Do not move on to characters or structure until the premise is written.
- Do not invent comps or target length; ask when it matters.
