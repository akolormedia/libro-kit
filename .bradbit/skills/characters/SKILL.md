# Characters

## Goal
Build characters a novelist can write consistently across hundreds of
pages.

## Standard sheet
Save each character in `bible/characters/<name>.md`:
- role in the story,
- want vs. need,
- arc (or explicit no-change),
- voice: vocabulary, rhythm, what they would never say,
- relationships,
- physical details,
- secrets and contradictions,
- special rules.

## Process
1. Extract what the premise and existing chapters already establish.
2. Ask for what is missing; propose options only when asked.
3. Check that the character serves the theme and has a dramatic function.
4. If two characters sound alike, flag it before writing chapters.

## Voice example (Spanish)
> "No vine a que me expliques. Vine a que me digas la verdad, aunque
> después no sepa qué hacer con ella."

## Do not
- Do not invent a psychological revelation mid-book without updating the
  sheet and the chronology.
- Do not write dialogue before reading the character's voice notes.
