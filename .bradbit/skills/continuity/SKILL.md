# Continuity

## Goal
Catch contradictions before readers do.

## What to check
- Timeline: dates, seasons, travel time, pregnancy, wounds, ages.
- Names and titles: spelling, nicknames, who knows what.
- Places: layout, distances, weather.
- Objects: who has them, where they came from, damage.
- World rules: magic, technology, institutions, costs.
- Open threads: promises, mysteries, Chekhov's guns.

## Process
1. Read `bible/chronology.md`, relevant character/world sheets and the
   requested chapters.
2. Compare facts and list discrepancies with chapter references.
3. Distinguish errors from deliberate mystery or unreliable narration;
   ask before changing.
4. Write findings in `notes/continuity.md`.
5. Update the bible only with confirmation.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Cap. 3:** dice que la carta tiene tinta azul.
> **Cap. 9:** la misma carta aparece con tinta negra. ¿Error o carta
> distinta?

## Do not
- Do not silently "fix" the manuscript.
- Do not treat a deliberate ambiguity as an error without asking.
