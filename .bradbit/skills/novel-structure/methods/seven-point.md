# Seven-point story structure (Dan Wells)

## What it is
A compact plot structure built around two turning points and a midpoint.
Popular for novels because it is easy to outline and revise.

## Points
1. **Hook:** the status quo and the promise of the story.
2. **Plot turn 1:** the call to action; the world changes.
3. **Pinch 1:** pressure is applied; the antagonist/obstacle shows its
   power.
4. **Midpoint:** the protagonist moves from reaction to action; a
   revelation or reversal raises the stakes.
5. **Pinch 2:** the worst pressure; allies are lost or the plan fails.
6. **Plot turn 2:** the final piece is found; the protagonist decides to
   act.
7. **Resolution:** the climax and the new status quo.

## When it works best
Genre novels, thrillers, fantasy and books with a strong external plot.
Pair it with a character arc to avoid a plot-only outline.
