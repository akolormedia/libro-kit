# Revision

## Goal
Improve the book with honest, prioritized notes — not a silent rewrite.

## Passes
1. **Structural:** premise, stakes, plot logic, character arc, pacing,
   chapter function, ending.
2. **Scene:** POV, goal, conflict, turn, entry/exit, exposition.
3. **Line:** prose, dialogue, repetition, clichés, rhythm, clarity.
4. **Proofreading:** grammar, punctuation, consistency.

## Notes format
Write in `notes/`:
- what works,
- what to revise (3-5 priorities),
- specific examples with chapter references,
- questions for the author,
- recommended next step.

## Example (Spanish)
> **A revisar:** el capítulo 12 resuelve la duda de Julia en una línea;
> falta el coste emocional de creerle a su madre.

## Do not
- Do not rewrite passages as if they were final.
- Do not give 20 equal-weight notes; prioritize.
