# Pitch

## Goal
Explain the book clearly to an agent, editor or reader.

## Pieces
- **Logline:** one sentence: protagonist, want, obstacle, stakes.
- **Synopsis:** 1 page, present tense, spoilers and ending included.
- **Query letter:** hook, book metadata (title, genre, word count,
  comps), short synopsis, author bio, closing.
- **Comps:** 2-3 recent titles with a clear "why".

## Process
1. Read `bible/premise.md`, `structure/outline.md`, the manuscript and
   reviews.
2. Write the logline first; if it is fuzzy, the book's stakes are fuzzy.
3. Write the synopsis in order, including the ending.
4. Write the query letter; keep it under one page.
5. Save `pitch/synopsis.md` and `pitch/query-letter.md`.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Logline:** una restauradora descubre las cartas de una mujer acusada
> de herejía y debe elegir entre publicarlas o proteger el secreto de su
> familia.

## Do not
- Do not hide the ending in the synopsis.
- Do not invent comps, awards or bio credits.
